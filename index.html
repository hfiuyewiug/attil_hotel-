About Attil Non Veg Hotel

Welcome to Attil Non Veg Hotel, located at Bendoorwell Main Road, Kankanady, Mangaluru.

Founded with a passion for authentic coastal Karnataka non-vegetarian traditions, Attil brings together time-tested family recipes, fresh daily-sourced meats, and rich coastal spice blends.

✓ 100% Halal and fresh meat sourced every morning.
✓ Fresh coastal fish directly from Mangalore harbour.
✓ Zero artificial food coloring or preservatives.
✓ Highest hygiene standards in preparation and dining.

Whether you are dining in with family or taking home your favorite biryani and curries, we promise food that delights your soul every single time!
